# Academic Journey: B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering

### Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)

This repository contains the implementation of various algorithms, system designs, and projects completed during my undergraduate studies at the **Department of Computer Science & Engineering**. The curriculum is based on a **Course System** requiring the successful completion of a minimum of **160.00 credits**.

## 🎓 Academic Context

- **Degree**: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Engineering (B.Sc. Engg.)
- **Institution**: Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)
- **Curriculum Basis**: RUET Undergraduate Studies – CSE Department  
- **Credit Requirement**: 160 credits over 8 semesters  

The curriculum emphasizes:
- Strong theoretical foundations  
- Hands-on sessional and laboratory work  
- Gradual transition from fundamentals to real-world engineering problems  

## 📚 Curriculum Overview

The undergraduate program is structured into four academic years, each divided into **Odd** and **Even** semesters.
